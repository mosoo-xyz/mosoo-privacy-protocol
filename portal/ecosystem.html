Real-World Privacy for
Individuals & Institutions.

Privacy is not about evasion—it is the basic prerequisite for fair markets, enterprise adoption, and personal safety. Without privacy, public blockchains cannot replace traditional banking.

The Fundamental Problem

Why Total Transparency Fails in the Real World

Imagine if your credit card statement, monthly salary, and mortgage balance were printed on a neon sign outside your front door for anyone to see. No business could negotiate supplier rates, no company could keep staff salaries confidential, and no individual would feel safe walking down the street.

Yet that is exactly how public blockchains operate today: every wallet address reveals its entire history, portfolio balance, and counterparties forever. Mosoo restores the privacy you already take for granted in everyday commerce.

Four Everyday Applications

💼

Confidential Web3 Payroll & Invoicing

When a DAO or blockchain company pays its contributors today, everyone can look up an employee's salary and past savings simply by checking their wallet on a block explorer.

With Mosoo, companies can fund a private pool once and distribute wages directly to contributors' shielded viewing keys. Employees receive their compensation without their total net worth or spending patterns being broadcast to coworkers or competitors.

✔ Zero on-chain salary leaks • Multi-UTXO payouts
🛡️

Anti-MEV & Institutional Trading

Automated arbitrage bots and "sandwich" searchers monitor public mempools 24/7. When a trader places a sizable order, bots front-run the trade and extract slippage profit at the trader's expense.

Mosoo routes trades through zero-knowledge state changes and gasless relayers. Because order amounts and token directions are encrypted inside Poseidon notes, searcher bots cannot inspect or sandwich the trade.

✔ Complete sandwich bot immunity • Zero frontrunning
🔒

Personal Financial Safety & Physical Security

If you pay a friend for dinner or buy an item from a local merchant using crypto, they now have your wallet address. They can inspect your balance, see your other transactions, and see every asset you own.

By holding funds in Mosoo notes, your main savings remain completely invisible. When transacting publicly, you simply unshield the exact required amount to a fresh address, leaving zero link to your primary wealth.

✔ Unlinkable peer-to-peer transfers • Cash-like digital hygiene
📜

Selective Compliance via Viewing Keys

Early privacy systems were condemned because they were total black boxes: you either revealed everything to the world or proved nothing to anyone.

Mosoo separates Spending Authority from Read-Only Auditability. You can export a cryptographic Stealth Viewing Key to share with your tax accountant, auditor, or legal regulator. They can independently verify your funds and tax liability without gaining the power to spend your coins.

✔ Read-only cryptographic audits • Regulatory peace of mind

The Independent Relayer Network

Mosoo does not rely on a single central server to submit transactions. Anyone with a node and native ABEY can operate a Relayer Daemon. Relayers compete on speed and reliability to broadcast verified zero-knowledge proofs to AbeyChain miners.

⚡

Trustless Architecture

Relayers cannot change your withdrawal address, alter the amount, or steal your tokens. If they modify a single byte, the smart contract's proof verification reverts immediately.

🛡️

Pre-Flight Simulation

Relayer nodes simulate transactions via staticCall before broadcasting on-chain, ensuring zero gas is wasted on invalid transactions or malicious mempool spam.

💰

Sustainable Economics

Operators earn a small network fee (typically 0.1% to 0.3%) deducted directly from the unshielded note, aligning incentives between users and node operators.
