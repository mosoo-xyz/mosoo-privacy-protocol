Protocol / Architecture / How It Works

Zero-Knowledge Privacy,
Explained Plainly.

Public blockchains broadcast every wallet balance, transfer, and transaction timestamp to anyone with an internet connection. Mosoo uses zero-knowledge cryptography to break this link forever. Here is how the engine works from deposit to withdrawal.

The Everyday Analogy

The Coat-Check Ticket System

Imagine checking your coat into a secure cloakroom at a concert hall. You hand over your coat, and the attendant hands you a plastic claim ticket stamped with a unique random number. Your name, home address, and face are not recorded anywhere. Hours later, you (or a friend you handed the ticket to) return to the counter, show the ticket, and receive the coat. The attendant has no idea who originally brought it in—only that the claim ticket is genuine.

Mosoo works the exact same way with money: You deposit public ABEY into the contract. Your browser creates a cryptographic claim ticket (called a Note) using a secret salt. You can hold it, send it privately to someone else, or cash it out to a clean address whenever you want. The blockchain can verify that the ticket exists, without ever knowing who owns it.

The 3 Core Operations of Mosoo

01

Shielding (Deposit into the Anonymity Pool)

You connect your standard Web3 wallet (such as MetaMask) and deposit native ABEY or supported tokens into the MosooHardenedPool contract. Before the transaction leaves your browser, your local device generates a cryptographic commitment:
Commitment = Poseidon(ViewingKey, SecretSalt, Amount)
Only this hash is stored on-chain inside a 20-level Merkle tree. Your secret salt never touches the network.

Public On-Chain Event
Shield(commitment, leafIndex, amount)
Identity Leak
0.0% (Receiver identity undisclosed)
02

Shielded Private Transfers (Holding & Moving Privately)

Once inside the pool, your funds are divided into encrypted UTXO notes (similar to physical cash bills). When you send funds to someone else, you don't send tokens to their public 0x address. Instead, you send them a newly encrypted note using their Stealth Viewing Key.
Your browser generates a client-side zero-knowledge SNARK proof. This proof mathematically proves: "I own an existing note in the Merkle tree, and I am consuming it to create a new note for the recipient, with the exact same total value."

Cryptographic Circuit
Groth16 BN254 (18,420 Constraints)
Public Trail
Zero address trail — fully severed
03

Unshielding (Clean Exit to Public Blockchain)

When you are ready to withdraw funds back to the public ledger, you choose any recipient address—ideally a brand-new, clean address that has never interacted with your identity.
To prevent double-spending, your browser publishes a unique Nullifier Hash derived from the secret salt: Nullifier = Poseidon(SecretSalt, LeafIndex). The contract checks its nullifierHashes registry. If the nullifier is unseen, the funds are paid out, and the nullifier is permanently marked spent.

Crucial Privacy Mechanics

Why Gasless Relayers Are Essential

Here is a common pitfall with legacy privacy systems: Suppose you withdraw 100 ABEY to a clean, fresh wallet address. To submit that withdrawal transaction to the blockchain, that wallet needs gas money (native ABEY).

If you send 0.5 ABEY from your old Binance or MetaMask account to fund that new wallet's gas, blockchain surveillance firms immediately connect both addresses. Your privacy is instantly destroyed before you even withdraw.

How Mosoo solves this: Our decentralized Relayer network broadcasts the withdrawal transaction for you and pays the gas on-chain. In exchange, a tiny relayer fee is deducted directly from the note being withdrawn. Your fresh wallet receives clean ABEY without ever needing a single drop of gas from an old address.

The Three Mathematical Pillars

🌳

20-Level Merkle Tree

Every note commitment is inserted as a leaf in an incremental binary tree. A depth of 20 allows the pool to hold 1,048,576 notes without slowing down.

PoseidonT3 Hasher on BN254
🔄

64-Root Ring Buffer

When multiple people deposit at the same time, the tree root changes. Our contract remembers the last 64 historical roots, so your transaction never fails due to network race conditions.

Historical Cache Invariant
⚡

Client-Side Groth16 Prover

Your private keys never leave your machine. Zero-knowledge proofs are calculated directly inside your web browser via WebAssembly in less than 3 seconds.
