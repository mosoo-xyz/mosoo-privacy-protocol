HOW PRIVACY WORKS

Financial Confidentiality in 3 Clear Steps

No complex math degrees required. Here is how you turn public blockchain transactions into mathematically untraceable transfers on AbeyChain.

🛡️
STEP 01

Shield (Deposit)

Deposit native ABEY into the Hardened Pool. Your browser generates a secret note Poseidon(ViewingKey, SecretSalt, Amount). Only the cryptographic hash is placed into the Merkle tree.

Public Link Broken
⚡
STEP 02

Shielded Transfer

Send shielded notes to another user's Stealth Viewing Key. A browser-generated Groth16 zero-knowledge proof verifies the transfer without revealing your address, amount, or counterparties.

Zero Public Footprint
🔓
STEP 03

Unshield (Clean Exit)

Withdraw funds to a clean, unlinked wallet address. Independent gasless relayers broadcast your withdrawal so you never need to fund gas from an old public exchange account.

Trace Permanently Severed
